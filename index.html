Questions about Tabkept

What people ask before they install a tab tool. Anything else goes to the issue list. Ask on GitHub

Add “Tabkept: Session Saver & Tab Backup”?

It can:

  • Read your browsing history
  • Manage your downloads
CancelAdd extension

Why does Chrome say it can read my browsing history?

Saving a tab means storing its address and title. They stay in this browser and in backup files on your computer.

What does a sleeping tab do?

It keeps its place, title and icon, frees its memory and loads when you click it.

Where are my saves kept?

In this browser, and every hour in a file in Downloads/Tabkept. Removing Tabkept leaves the files.

Can it bring back a tab's back button history?

No. Chrome doesn't let extensions restore it, so a tab starts at the page it was on.

Does it save incognito windows?

Only if you turn that on and allow Tabkept in Incognito. They come back as private windows.
