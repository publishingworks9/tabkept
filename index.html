When Chrome restores nothing

The session from before is one click away.

A crash, a forced update, the wrong profile, a window closed by accident. Tabkept keeps the last session from before Chrome restarted and offers it in the popup while its tabs aren't open.

Restored tabs open asleep and load when you click them, so getting back 400 tabs doesn't freeze the computer.

Backup files

Files that are yours, not the extension's.

Once an hour Tabkept writes a file to Downloads/Tabkept, with a page of links that opens in any browser. A reset Chrome, a reinstall or a new laptop costs nothing: the welcome page restores from the file.

Tab groups

Groups come back as you left them.

Names, colours, order and whether a group was collapsed are saved with every change. Restoring next to a group that's already open adds to it instead of making a second one.

Promises

What Tabkept will not do.

These are the reasons people give for leaving other tab tools. Each one is a rule in the code, checked by its tests.

  • Ask you to sign upThere is no account
  • Send your tabs anywhereNo server, no analytics, no network requests
  • Take over your new-tab pageOr pin a helper tab, or rename your tabs
  • Close a tab without a way backEverything it closes goes to a 30-day bin
  • Nag youNo donation screens, rating prompts or promotions
  • Take away what is freeEverything free today stays free

Why does Chrome say it can "read your browsing history"?

Tabkept needs the address and title of each open tab to save and restore it, and that is what this warning covers. The information stays in this browser and in the backup files on your computer. Tabkept does not read what is on pages.

What does a sleeping tab do?

It keeps its address, title and icon in the tab strip and frees the memory it used. It loads again when you click it. Tabkept uses Chrome's own tab sleeping, so nothing breaks if Tabkept is turned off or removed.

Where are my saves kept?

In this browser's storage, and once an hour in a file in Downloads/Tabkept. Removing Tabkept deletes the saves inside the browser; the files in Downloads stay until you delete them.

Can it bring back a tab's back and forward history?

No. Chrome doesn't let extensions restore that history, so a restored tab starts at the page it was on.

Does it save incognito windows?

Not unless you turn that on in Settings and allow Tabkept in Incognito. Private windows are only ever restored as private windows.

Can I bring my saved tabs from another tool?

Yes. Tabkept imports exported session files (JSON), CSV files, saved lists of links, Chrome bookmark files and its own backups. It can also send tabs left behind by a removed tab-sleeping extension back to their real pages.
