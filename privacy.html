Privacy policy

Tabkept for Chrome. Last updated 18 November 2026.

The short version

Tabkept keeps a copy of your open tabs on your computer and nowhere else. It has no account, no server and no analytics, and it makes no network requests of its own.

What Tabkept stores

  • The address, title and icon of each open tab, and whether it is pinned.
  • Your tab groups: name, colour, order and whether they are collapsed.
  • The size and position of each window, and any names you give windows in Tabkept.
  • Sessions you save, the automatic save timeline, and items in Tabkept's bin.
  • Your Tabkept settings.

Tabkept does not read what is on web pages, what you type, cookies, passwords or your browsing history beyond the tabs that are open. Incognito windows are not saved unless you turn that on in Settings and allow Tabkept in Incognito.

Where it is stored

Inside your Chrome profile on this computer, and, once an hour, in backup files that Tabkept writes to a folder called Tabkept in your Downloads folder. The backup files are ordinary files on your computer. Anyone who can open your Downloads folder can read them, and they stay after you remove the extension.

What Tabkept never does

  • It never sends your data anywhere. Nothing is uploaded, synced to a server or shared.
  • It does not sell, rent or transfer data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising, credit or lending decisions.
  • It contains no analytics, trackers or remote code.

Deleting your data

Settings > "Delete all saved data" removes every save, session, bin item and setting from the browser. Removing the extension does the same. Backup files in Downloads/Tabkept are not touched by either; delete them yourself if you want them gone.

Links to other sites

Tabkept's settings page may link to its source code on GitHub and to pages where you can sponsor or tip its development. Clicking one opens that site in a normal tab, under that site's own privacy policy. Tabkept sends nothing to those sites.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date before the change ships. Questions: use the Support tab of Tabkept's Chrome Web Store page.
