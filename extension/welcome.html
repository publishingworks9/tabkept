Tabkept

Keep a copy of every tab, as you work.

Tabkept saves your open windows a couple of seconds after anything changes, so a crash, an update or a closed window costs you nothing. Before it starts, here is exactly what it keeps.

What it saves
The address and title of each open tab, your tab groups (name, colour, order) and where each window sits on screen.
Where it goes
Into this browser's storage, and once an hour into a file in your Downloads folder, in a folder called Tabkept. The files stay yours if you remove the extension.
Hourly files
While it writes the hourly file, Chrome's download bubble stays hidden for a moment so it does not interrupt you. Your own downloads are not affected.
What it never does
Send anything anywhere. There is no account, no analytics and no server. Incognito windows are not saved unless you turn that on.

New computer, or reinstalled Chrome? Bring back your sessions from a Tabkept backup file (Downloads/Tabkept) or another tool's export.
