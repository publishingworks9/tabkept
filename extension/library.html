Delete all saved data

Removes every save, session, bin item and setting from this browser and stops saving until you start it again. Backup files already in Downloads/Tabkept stay; delete them there if you want them gone too.

What Tabkept can access, and why

Your open tabs
Their addresses, titles and icons, to save and restore them. Chrome calls this "read your browsing history".
Tab groups
Names, colours and whether they are collapsed, to bring groups back as they were.
Storage
Saves, sessions, the bin and settings, kept in this browser.
Downloads
To write backup files to Downloads/Tabkept and remove its own oldest ones, and to keep the download bubble quiet during hourly backups.
Alarms
To check saving every minute, write the hourly backup and run the sleep timer.
Right-click menu
Sleep this tab, sleep the other tabs, never sleep this site, save this window.
Site icons
Shown from Chrome's own icon cache, so no icon is fetched from a website.
Background page
Turns a very large backup into a file Chrome can download.

Tabkept does not ask for access to the content of any website.

What Tabkept will not do

  • Ask you to sign up. There is no account.
  • Send anything anywhere. It makes no network requests and has no analytics.
  • Replace your new-tab page, pin a helper tab, or change tab titles.
  • Show donation screens, ratings prompts or promotional notifications.
  • Close a tab without putting it in the bin first.
  • Take away anything that is free today.

Saves stay in this browser; backup files are ordinary files in your Downloads folder.
